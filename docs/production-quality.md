# Integrated Production Quality (F-06 candidate)

Opt in with project.json `"productionQuality": {}`. Defaults: target -16 LUFS, maximum true peak -1.5 dBTP, 64 characters/cue, 28 characters/second, minimum cue .35 s; optional exportCrf 16–28 (null retains existing stream-copy final assembly). Existing projects remain compatible. Reprepare/review/freeze after enabling; capture marker contract and caption/preview inputs are versioned separately.

## Capture, voice and captions
Production capture records monotonic action indexes/start/end relative to browser page creation, plus declared evidence IDs. These are approximate video-time markers, not frame-accurate synchronization. Capture is still actual browser footage and required assertions still must pass. The voice master uses FFmpeg measured loudness, bounded gain and stereo PCM at 48 kHz. It preserves original selected voice and duration; no trim, time stretch, voice cloning, music or paid synthesis. If loudness and true peak cannot both meet the limits, fail rather than distort speech. Cache master by input bytes and mastering settings. Preview QA also measures final AAC loudness/peak. Silence without voice remains valid. Caption text remains in the original script language; Unicode character count, duration, CPS and text completeness are checked, not ASR or translation. Inferred/manual timing still needs listening review.

## Composition and evidence
Remotion captions scale to viewport; overlays and existing camera primitives remain available. For claim-bearing scenes, the conservative temporal check requires continuous 1x capture playback from time zero through the recorded action sequence plus a small guard interval. It rejects trims/reorder/fades that can hide that interval; this intentionally restricts advanced cuts under this first quality policy. It does not prove visible UI after zoom, overlay or crop, semantic truth, or exact marker-to-frame correspondence. Up to eight JPEG review candidates are extracted from actual preview, with timestamps and hashes. Review frames and render/master receipt are checked again at QA/approval/final assembly. Frame candidates are NOT a semantic review or an automatic pass on artistic quality. Agent/operator must inspect them and actual video/audio. No independent shot rendering or true crossfade system is claimed.

## Final export
Existing scene QA and approvals remain required. The output includes final.mp4, final-qa.json, final.srt and final-report.json with measured scene-preview offsets, scene/claim IDs, media/QA hashes and estimated provider reservations. Final QA binds both sidecars by hash. Offsets use probed scene durations; not forced alignment or verified exact concatenated frame timing. Optional exportCrf applies an extra H.264/AAC encode only for FFmpeg final assembly; choose explicitly when needed, otherwise retain stream copy. No actual billing, H.265, release immutability or semantic approval inference.

## Validation
Run npm test and the existing AVP_INTEGRATED_SMOKE=1 suite. Unit tests exercise Unicode captions, marker coverage and real FFmpeg gain mastering; integrated dashboard/canvas use the opt-in contract and actual capture, edited preview, QA, final audit and scoped repair. Managed fixture checks no-voice mode. Synthetic audio and fixture approvals are not natural speech or independent LLM quality evaluation. CI success required before merge. All remaining B2/B3/B4 criteria stay open until their specific evidence exists.
